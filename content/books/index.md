## [From Noise to Knowing](https://bmrtech-oss.github.io/antinode-research/)

_The Discipline of Turning Information into Knowledge_ — Pramod Rathod

How do we turn the flood of information around us into knowledge we can trust and use? This book explores that process, from separating signal from noise to testing and integrating what we learn.
